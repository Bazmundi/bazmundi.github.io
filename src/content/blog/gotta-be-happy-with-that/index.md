I may or may not have mentioned, my MonstaPC was down with the lurgy, meaing I got bitten by the MSI liquid cooler recall a few months ago. MSI how outsourced their radiator production, and the supplier hadn't cleaned the radiators after making them (and I guess no means to receipt inspect items by MSI), so they eventually clagged up.



I originally approached MWave to replace the problematic cooler, cause that's who I purchased the cooler through. However, the hoops they wanted me to go through, even though the MSI website verified my cooler serial number was in the recall, tich.



Turns out, it was straight forward to send it back to MSI. They provided a paid courier service etc.



I had to wait as they didn't have any 280R on the shelf. I was not in a hurry, so I waited and a V2 unit turned up. That was a few or so months ago.



I finally got breathing space to put it back together and so I am now typing this post out on the MonstaPC.



The replacement cooler is humming away, the temps are way down on what I was seeing, even before the cooler fully fragged itself.



The PC is much happier now, me too.
