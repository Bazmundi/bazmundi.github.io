Hmmm. Just came across an [open source tool changer](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3880296). So, if my basic idea for a magnetic tool head doesn't pan out then I have a fall back option. Though, this build doesn't really warrant anything to exotic.



[![](./updated-idea36-1.png)](./updated-idea36-1.png)

Does it need to be more complicated than this?



Though, I wasn't really setting up for multiple heads per se, though the idea of dropping a laser in (occasionally) did cross my mind.



The question in my mind is still around the pull force of the magnets, and if any pulling is required (tapes, or pushpullfeeder increments), will this idea fail? Exciting stuff. We'll find out.



The main reason I am using the magnets is, well there's actually two reasons, 1) the spatial constraints make it tricky to arrange this so as not to have problems with the Z-Axis mounting holes; 2) fun with magnets and to see if they can solve this problem.



I still think I may (I say may) have problems with this idea, as the constraints on the central pin appear to result in the body colliding with the pin, if levering from the left end (rotating from the right end). That could be solved with a shorter pin etc. It depends on the air gap and the pull strength curve.
