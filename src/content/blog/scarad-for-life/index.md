Long thinking about a SCARA, but that fell into the background when I started to build my PnP machines.



However, then came a 3D printed SCARA that, I think, is [precisely the way it should be done](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL2_HQm7IFtrA3KNlGg0bzj_UPydvFgIM3).
