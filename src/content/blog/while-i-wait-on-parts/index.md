So, I thought I would set up a Pi with my BIGTREETECH SKR Mini e3 v2.0 with Klipper and started poking around, looking for guides etc.



Thankyou Klipper team for a [config file](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Klipper3d/klipper/master/config/generic-bigtreetech-skr-mini-e3-v2.0.cfg)!



I noted you apparently had to install Octaprint on your Pi to set up some aspects, but didn't need use Octaprint per se. But not sure why you wouldn't? If you had Gggrunt!



I have a 3B+ that I thought I would use, but then I remembered I had lucked into a Raspberry Pi 2 W and wondered ...


[![](./images.jpg)](./images.jpg)

Gggrunt!




... and apparently Rasperry Pi 2 W [with the quad core is exactly what you need for the job](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l7gjxqTY1Rk), but [hints on installation are here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtW3GqkKUz8&t=254s).



So I started with [the destructions here](https://www.klipper3d.org/Installation.html).



BUT ...



Stopped half way through the download of Octaprint BECAUSE I drifted to the Octaprint website, ...


[![](./me-on-the-net.png)](./me-on-the-net.png)



... while waiting for the dowload of Octaprint, per the destructions, I found a [prepackaged Octaprint/Klipper bundle](https://github.com/guysoft/OctoKlipperPi/releases).



STOP!



RESTART!



A little more poking around lead me onto a [wide angle pi camera idea](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9hj-LkIQs48&t=93s), and so I ordered one of those from [Littlebird Electronics](https://littlebirdelectronics.com.au/products/camera-module-for-raspberry-pi-zero-160-variable-focus).



Just need me a pesky 32G SD card, now where are they hiding ... nope, off to Jaycar then.



In the meantime, I am 3D printing a mount for the [RPi Zero 2](https://www.printables.com/model/222518-raspberry-pi-zero-2w-mount-for-2040-aluminium-prof), and also another for the [BIGTREETECH SKR Mini e3 v2.0](https://www.printables.com/model/172510-bigtreetech-skr-mini-e3-v2030-bracket/files).
