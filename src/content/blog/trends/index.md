I was looking into limitations brought by the [Android Mini PC](http://organicmonkeymotion.wordpress.com/2012/10/04/gumstix-killer/ "Gumstix Killer") to find there aren't as many as I thought.
They don't come with Bluetooth but have wireless, a micro USB and a host USB port.   The real limitation I thought they had, initially, is needing a host port on the Arduino boards to talk to it.
I learned recently about [On The Go](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USB_On-The-Go), a USB variant, which allows an Android device to talk to any USB device (and presumably a serial port, we still have to see about that).
There is also a late breaking [Ubuntu port](http://liliputing.com/2012/12/now-you-can-install-ubuntu-on-the-ug802-mk802-iii-or-other-rk3066-mini-pcs.html) for the device I have in my drawer so a couple of options.
And, of course, now we have a [BeagleBoard/BeagleBone](http://cubieboard.org/), and let's face it Raspberry Pi, Killer.
