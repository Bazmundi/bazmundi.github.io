While I am starting out with a manual pnp, I am still playing around with what if for an automatic pnp.



I have had some inspiration watching [Stephen Hawes](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCMf49SMPnhxdLormhEpfyfg) and his enthusiastic process of designing the *[index](https://github.com/index-machines)*.



But I have had a poke around and I am very interested in CoreXY mechanisms and have been looking at whether there are diy versions of CoreXY that might be repurposed as a pnp design.



To wit, I think we're there with the [Rat-Rig](https://github.com/Rat-Rig), especially with a [store stood up](https://www.ratrig.com/) and so some of the special non-printed parts (pulleys and plates and all).



Although, there are alternatives such as [this](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4762541) or [this](http://makerhardware.net/wiki/doku.php?id=manuals:core_xy) or [this](https://github.com/alexyu132/fusebox3).
