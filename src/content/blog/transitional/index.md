... ideas.



So, I am playing more with transitions between "scenes", in my little video ditties.



So, I am super attentive now, when watching movies, of ideas for transitions.



Noting, however, swirly, whirly transitions and other high maintenance transitions are not the norm.



Two transitions I picked up on, that give me ideas, are:



* A scene change in the Alienist, where one scene was someone investigating a kidnap scene, a house with a child's room with a rocking horse. In that scene, the investigator, while in deep thought, set the rocking horse to gentle rocking. Then a cut to a scene in a toy store, actually the source of a doll that was left by the abductor at the scene, with the briefest flitting of a child running through the store, as the camera panned, and a rocking horse set rocking, by the passing child.
* Opening scenes in A Quiet Place II, John Karinski's character, grabbing fruit from a store, a single apple and a bag otherwise of oranges, leaves the store, takes a bite of the apple, change of scene, and apple core dropped in a bin, an action almost lost in the wash of everything else going on in the scene—but there. So, might be called continuity but, something of an L-cut, I think.
