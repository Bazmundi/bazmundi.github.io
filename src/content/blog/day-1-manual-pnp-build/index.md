So, went to maker space to start the [manual pnp build](https://organicmonkeymotion.wordpress.com/2021/03/02/picky-picky-picky/).



Someone was using the PLA printer so I jumped onto an ABS printer to print the axis pieces. A 10hr print so left it running overnight to pick up tomorrow.



Started out to assemble the aluminium frame. Crap! So the Aliexpress vendor I had trouble with, getting the extrusion order correct, did me in again! I ordered L-shaped corner braces. I noticed through the bubble wrap metal plates so felt I had received at least those L-shaped pieces. Once I opened up the wrap I found T-shaped and therefore not corner braces. DOH!



Secondly, while those sexy drop-in T nuts seemed a good idea, they were crap as they generally failed to rotate into position and so I gave up assembling the frame, for the moment. I have now ordered standard T nuts for the build, from interstate so as to get them before next weekend. I also ordered L-shaped corner pieces. I will keep the T-shaped pieces aside for another project.



I did toy with the idea of going without the corner braces, since with 3 x 2020 and 2 x 2080 pieces across the frame, why the additional corner braces were felt necessary is beyond me. But I relented and bought 4, rather than print them since I was ordering the other bits.



I also ordered a batch of cast corner connectors to replace the ones I got from Aliexpress. Yeah, my fault that one. The pnp design actually depends upon corner connectors of a certain size. Since, the tape dispensers are designed to span two 2020 sections, spaced by a corner connector, and therefore a 20mm span. The corner connecters I got from Aliexpress were a larger shape (>20mm) - but dirt cheap. I will find another job for them.



The upshot, the Day 1 build will be thrown out and started again once the new nuts, cast corner pieces and L-shaped corner braces turn up.



The other thing that became apparent is there is at least one stl file missing from the thingiverse site for the manual pnp - for some dang reason. No problem though as there is a step file of the thang also and you can import that into FreeCAD and select individual part to then export as STL.



UPDATE



Picked up me ABS print. Hmmm. Some shrinkage that split open a layer. It is likely fixable, I am told, using acetone. The top of one piece did not seem to get all its layers laid down. I suspect, as it was running over night, the layers involved were likely in and around a drop in temp of the space. May need a boost with a incubator heater as I have in my 11U rack for my Finder, to keep the temp around the print higher to help reduce the shrinkage. I may just reprint in PLA.
