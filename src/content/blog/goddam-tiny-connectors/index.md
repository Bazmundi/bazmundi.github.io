I've gone through about twenty PH 2.0mm connector pins trying to work out how to crimp those tiny suckers.



I had about a 10% success rate, practicing, having followed a range of tutorials on Youtube that all seemed to rely upon overcoming the tiny piece work somehow with finesse from somewhere, and a stupid amount of patience.



I had long worked out my SNA-28B was not the crimper for the smaller connectors, so got me a SN-2549B. However, the PH 2.0mm are so stinking tiny and fiddly I was crushing them, cutting the crimps off while crimping blah, blah, blah.



The crimper in the first video its an ADAFRUIT PA-09 which is close to a YE-20B from Aliexpress, so I ordered a YE-20B but soon cancelled when I found a second video. The YE-20B/PA-09 are a two pass crimping tool as you crimp the wire first then crimp the insulation.




https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jHfYzrSF4pY




The crimper in the second video was an SN-01B but that made sense as it limits the problem down to AWG 28-20 [although according to one site](https://www.wiringo.com/jst-cable-assembly.html) PH 2.0 is actually targeting AWG 32-24. In the second video the crimper was closer to me SN-2549B in style of jaw, and offers one step crimping.




https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dY3hbyF3ypA




In any event, whos to say as I used the AWG 28-26 site (0.8mm) of the SN-2549B and got a brilliant crimp of a JST PH 2.0mm connector. The selection was made because with the strip left on, butting the strip against one side of the jaw, the connector sat cleanly on the other side of the jaw at the AWG 28-26 site (0.8mm) location (the next site along the crimper jaws appeared too wide for the connector and would crush the connector).
