Gag!
I am in heaven.
Remember I talked about coding for [transputers in another life](http://organicmonkeymotion.wordpress.com/parallel-hardware/ "The good old days").
Well, there are a team that have developed occum-π for the Arduino - [Transterpreter](http://www.transterpreter.org/ "par(Heaven).start()").
Expect, it burns about 20k for the occam interpreter when running the LED blink example.
That's up there with Windows needing 250K (at least) to display hello world (in contrast its about 30 bytes in assembly).
Oh well. Interesting toy - and still 100k of "room" on my Arduino Mega board.
