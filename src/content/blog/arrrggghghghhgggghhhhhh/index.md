So many niggly things.



I had bought a hardware kit for a prusa printer on Aliexpress (screws, washers et al.). But, of course, there are differences between the prusa and the bear. So, missing were M5x10mm button heads and, of course, the one and only one blinking 3mm metal dowel of 15mm. It also turned out the kit did not include the idler for the blinking Y-Axis or X-Axis tensioner idlers either.



I found a local place that has the 3mm metal dowel, for $1 but with $10 flat rate postage .. WTF!?!?



I have M5x10mm button heads from my corexy PnP build, so I borrowed one. But ordered a bag of M4 and M5 button heads locally to have aside for spontaneous builds.



So a slow mail order from Aliexpress and the build goes back on the shelf until dowels and idlers turn up.
