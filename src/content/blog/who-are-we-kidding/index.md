And all we get back is [one animal](https://www.themonthly.com.au/issue/2018/august/1533045600/james-bradley/end-oceans#mtr) from the brink of extinction if we stop Chinese wet markets.

For how many thousands of years has this practice been ingrained not merely in the Chinese culture but in the identity of the Chinese people? There will be black wet, or if you like wet [black, markets](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2020/03/illegal-wildlife-trade-online-china-shuts-markets-200324040543868.html) on the other side of this.

We may never know since the Chinese government appears hell bent on continuing [the cover-up](https://7news.com.au/lifestyle/health-wellbeing/coronavirus-china-tightens-grip-over-covid-19-research-amid-row-with-us-on-virus-origin-c-972555).
