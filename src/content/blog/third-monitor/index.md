I've got two monitors that my Monsta PC and my trusty old HP Scectre X360 share. I was thinking of a third monitor for the Monsta PC (or even the laptop), especially a touch screen monitor. The touch screen or tablet based approach looks like a more natural way of sculpting in Blender.



That might appeal to me wife, maybe.



Several touchscreen monitors are known to work well with Ubuntu Linux due to their support in the Linux kernel. Here are some examples and key details:



* Dell P2418HT - A 24-inch FHD monitor with 10-point touch support. It's widely reported as Linux-compatible out of the box. Connection requires HDMI and USB for touch functionality.
* ASUS VT229H - A 21.5-inch FHD touchscreen that supports 10-point touch and has good Linux kernel support. It is compact and versatile for desktop setups.
* ViewSonic TD2230 - A 22-inch FHD 10-point touchscreen with sturdy construction, making it suitable for Linux systems, including Ubuntu.
* Acer T232HL - A 23-inch touch monitor with compatibility for Ubuntu. Users often find it functional without additional drivers, but USB is required for touch functionality.
