I thought I'd use the panel feature at PCBWay to panel small boards. Got knocked back as there's also a limit it seems on the lowest number of panels you can buy that way.



Seems if you want a small number of items in panels, you do need panel them in KiKit and then you'll get hit with the machining bits etc. Not unexpected but, it's all a bit weird as I fully expect they would make 5 would at least panel them amoungst a group of others, up to whatever the starting size is of a two sided board, and then batch them anyway.



Minimum panel style, surely, is say 5 boards joined by a v-cut or mouse bites? With no frame yadda.
