**Mobile Robot Course**
 [Introduction to Mobile Robotics (engl.) - Autonomous Mobile Systems](http://ais.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/teaching/ss09/robotics/ "It's in English, self study with Octave code examples").  You will need [Octave](http://www.gnu.org/software/octave/ "Download Octave from here") (the MATLAB clone).
Do read also Simultaneous Localisation and Mapping (SLAM): [part 1](http://www.doc.ic.ac.uk/~ajd/Robotics/RoboticsResources/SLAMTutorial1.pdf "SLAM part 1") and [part 2](http://www.doc.ic.ac.uk/~ajd/Robotics/RoboticsResources/SLAMTutorial2.pdf "SLAM part 2").
Handy reading if you are working with monster trucks as the basic robotic vehicle ([Ackermann Drive](http://www.rctek.com/technical/handling/ackerman_steering_principle.html "Oh, that's why the wheels look wobbly")).  Calculating [circular paths](http://rossum.sourceforge.net/papers/CalculationsForRobotics/CirclePath.htm "Circular path calculations") to meet with a waypoints.
A more [down to earth piece talks](http://ikalogic.com/wfr2.php "Close to home.") about waypoints, dead-reckoning et al.
Here is an interesting piece that talks about [subsumption](http://geology.heroy.smu.edu/~dpa-www/robo/subsumption/ "Short of BDI, what does one do to have optional behaviour?").  Same chap talks about [odometry](http://www.geology.smu.edu/~dpa-www/robo/Encoder/imu_odo/ "Tick tick tick").
