[BeagleBoard](http://elinux.org/BeagleBoard) begates [PandaBoard](http://pandaboard.org/).  [Raspberry Pi](http://www.raspberrypi.org/) verus [BeagleBone](http://beagleboard.org/static/beaglebone/latest/README.htm).   And now, finally [Cubiboard](http://cubieboard.org/) or [ODROID-U2](http://www.hardkernel.com/renewal_2011/products/prdt_info.php?g_code=G135341370451).
The prices are dropping and the prospect of a Beowulf cluster in a shoe box looms.  Perhaps for a [Beobot 3](http://ilab.usc.edu/beobot2/doku.php)?
