So, I find I can't even delete my discord account!



Why would I want to delete my discord account?



Well, fun with two-factor authentication.



It wants a phone number.



No, I was not averse to adding my phone number, I couldn't.



When I tried joining a new group I got the TFA prompt. I put my phone number in and got an SMS message.



The SMS message said my phone number was already in use so I had to delete that account and create a new one.



Problem is I only have one discord account. (Or so I thought, see UPDATE!)



I checked, my discord account did not have an phone number attached, so I tried to attach my phone number, but it would not let me. BECAUSE (apparently) my phone number is attached to another discord account.



I checked, I only have one other email account and it is not associated with a discord account.



Now yes, how do I know I don't have a second discord account attached to my second email address? Well, easy peasy, I asked for a password reset and discord is kind enough to tell me that email address is not in their system.



I am not inclined to add my phone numbers to accounts generally, and so my discord account did not have a phone number at all.



So, apparently if you send a help request on TFA to discord help they send an email with a blurb about phone authentication and why the problem is YOUR FAULT.



But, as I said at the beginning, even deleting accounts is fraught. You hit the DELETE button and the thing hangs. It seems to log you out but you can log back in again. But, even if deleting the account and creating a new one was an option, it isn't. It isn't because somewhere in discord they have my phone number attached to another account which is not attached to my second email address.



Impass.



So, I opted into telling discord help to delete my account since I don't seem to be able to. I also will opt out of discord as this is too much trouble as I don't use discord often enough. WHAT? What will I do to accumulate KUDOS? Who the fuck cares about KUDOS?



Fun fact, even if my phone number is attached to some rogue discord account, I am not getting any SMS traffic on that phone number from discord, as you would expect if it were attached to an account running TFA.



**UPDATE!**



So, I tried something different. Since discord felt my phone number was assigned to another account, I tried logging in by phone number. What fun, it popped up with "Hello Baz" which is on nickname I use occasionally. Long lost and long disused account therefore.



So, having logged in with phone number, discord wanted to authenticate with an email address so I tried my main then secondary email address. It accepted my secondary email address. So, the fun fact is the secondary email address was the one discord said was not associated with a discord account when I tried logging in with that secondary address, recalling I tried and failed trying to reset the password for that secondary email address.



That is:



* Tried to log in with secondary email address, discord is discordant.
* Ask to reset password on secondary email address, discord disavows knowledge of the secondary email address.
* Log in with phone number, discord happily accepts secondary email address as the verification email address and allows resetting of password (probably against the phone number but not the email address itself).



Weirdness remained, as I was able to delete the Baz account, even though delete would not work for my other discord account. Once Baz was deleted, I was able to add a phone number to my main account and also get into a server to ask a question - finally. Gone was 3-4 hours the day due to this investigation.
